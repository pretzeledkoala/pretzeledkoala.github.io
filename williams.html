  • Complex Analysis (MATH 383)
  • Advanced Analysis: C*-Algebras (MATH 389)
  • Commutative Algebra (MATH 411)

I also completed 3 reading courses:

  1. Local Fields & Root Systems (with Leo Goldmakher): Based on PAWS 2024 courses on symmetries of root systems and local fields.
  2. Geometry & Quantum Theory (with Ivo Terek): Read Dan Freed's Quantum Theory from a Geometric Viewpoint.
  3. Floer Homology (with Tom Garrity): Studied Morse and Floer theory toward Manolescu's disproof of the triangulation conjecture.
